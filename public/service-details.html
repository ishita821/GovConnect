Birth Certificate

Apply online for a Birth Certificate quickly and securely through Government Certificate Portal.

Description

Apply for a Birth Certificate online by submitting the required details and documents. Track your application status and download the approved certificate securely through the Government Certificate Portal.

Eligibility

  • Parents of the child
  • Legal Guardian
  • Adult Citizen

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Hospital Birth Record
  • Parent Identity Proof
  • Passport Size Photograph

Charges

Government Fee : ₹100

Service Charge : ₹50

Total : ₹150

Processing Time

3–7 Working Days

How to Apply

  1. Click Apply Now.
  2. Fill the application form.
  3. Upload required documents.
  4. Submit the application.
  5. Track your request.
